---
title: "Get a quote's status"
sidebarTitle: "Get quote status"
description: "Follow a quote through payment to its contract."
api: "GET /quote/{id}/status"
authMethod: "key"
---

Poll this after [paying a quote](/api-reference/quotes/pay) to know when the payment settles. Djigui does not notify your systems yet.

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `awaiting_payment` | Never paid, or every attempt failed. You can pay it. |
| `payment_pending` | Waiting for the payer to confirm in Wave. |
| `pending`, `started`, `expired` | Paid: the contract's status. `contractId` is set. |

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
  The quote's id.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="quoteId" type="string">
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="contractId" type="string | null">
  The contract, once paid. See [Get a contract](/api-reference/contracts/get).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="payment" type="object | null">
  The latest payment attempt, with the fields of [Pay a quote](/api-reference/quotes/pay#response). `null` if the quote was never paid.
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | `code` | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 404 | `quote_not_found` | No quote with this id in your company. |
