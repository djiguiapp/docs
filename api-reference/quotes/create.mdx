---
title: "Create a quote"
description: "Price a vehicle under a coverage, for a usage, duration and start date."
api: "POST /quote/create"
authMethod: "key"
---

Prices one of your vehicles, as registered, under a coverage. The quote can then be [paid](/api-reference/quotes/pay) until its start date.

You can create as many quotes as you need for the same vehicle: other coverages, durations or usages.

## Body

<ParamField body="vehicleId" type="string" required>
  One of your company's vehicles.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="coverageId" type="string" required>
  A coverage for the vehicle's type: a car coverage for an `auto`, a motorbike coverage for a `bike`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="duration" type="integer" required>
  `3`, `6` or `12` months.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="usage" type="string" required>
  How the vehicle is used under this contract: `pleasure` (private and business use) or `renting` (rental).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="policyHolderName" type="string" required>
  The policyholder's full name, 2 to 150 characters.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="policyHolderPhone" type="string" required>
  The policyholder's Ivorian mobile number: 10 digits starting with `01`, `05` or `07`, with or without `+225`, spaces allowed. Printed on the ASACI certificate. Returned as `+225` followed by the 10 digits.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="startDate" type="string" required>
  First day covered, `YYYY-MM-DD`, today or later.
</ParamField>

## Errors

| Status | `code` | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | `invalid_request` | A field breaks a rule, or `startDate` is before today. |
| 404 | `vehicle_not_found` | No vehicle with this id in your company. |
| 404 | `coverage_not_found` | No vehicle coverage with this id. |
| 422 | `coverage_not_available` | The coverage is for the other vehicle type, the vehicle is too old for it, or it cannot be priced through this API. `message` says which. |
