---
title: "List quotes"
description: "Your company's quotes, with their status, optionally for one vehicle."
api: "GET /quote"
authMethod: "key"
---

Returns your company's quotes, most recent first, [paginated](/pagination). Each quote has its current `status` and, once paid, its `contractId`.

## Query

<ParamField query="vehicleId" type="string">
  Only this vehicle's quotes.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="page" default="1" type="integer">
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="20">
  1 to 100.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="data" type="object[]">
  Quotes, with the fields of [Create a quote](/api-reference/quotes/create#response).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="page" type="integer">
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="limit" type="integer">
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="total" type="integer">
</ResponseField>
