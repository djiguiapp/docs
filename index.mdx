The Djigui Business API lets your company take out vehicle insurance on behalf of your clients. You register a client's vehicle, get a price for the coverage they want, have them pay with Wave, and follow the resulting contract until its ASACI certificate is issued.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a coverage">
    List the coverages Djigui sells and what each one covers. Prices are not listed: they depend on the vehicle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the vehicle">
    Send the vehicle's details once, with its registration document if you have it. You can quote the same vehicle as many times as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a quote">
    Price the vehicle under a coverage, for 3, 6 or 12 months, for a given usage and start date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the payment">
    Open a Wave payment for the quote. The payer confirms it from their Wave app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the contract">
    Once paid, the quote becomes a contract. Djigui requests the ASACI certificate and gives you its links and documents as soon as it is issued.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Base URL

Every endpoint is under:

```text
https://businessapi-staging-civ.djigui.io/api/v1.0
```

<Note>
  This is the staging environment: build and test your integration against it. Djigui gives you the production URL and key when you go live, where payments are real Wave payments.
</Note>

## Conventions

- Requests and responses are JSON, except for [vehicle registration](/api-reference/vehicles/create) with a document, which also accepts `multipart/form-data`.
- Amounts are integers in francs CFA (XOF).
- Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`. Timestamps (`created`, `updated`, `completed`) are ISO 8601 in UTC.
- Ids are UUIDs.
- Unknown fields in a request body are refused with a `400`. Send only the documented fields.
- Everything you create belongs to your company: you never see another company's data, and their ids answer `404`.
